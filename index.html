A voice-first way to meet someone

No photos. No profiles.
Just voices.

The Voice Between Us is a voice-first way to meet someone. A matchmaker gets to know you by talking with you — five days of real conversation — then introduces you to one person, by voice. Names and faces stay hidden until you both choose to reveal them.

Two voices. One conversation. Nothing else in the way.

How it works

Three steps. No swiping, no feeds, no performing for a profile.

1

Talk

Each day, a short voice conversation with your matchmaker — what you love, how you live, what you're looking for.

2

Meet one voice

When the matchmaker finds your person, you start talking — voice only, no photos, no names.

3

Reveal together

When you're both ready, the veil lifts — together, or not at all.

Why voice

A voice carries what a profile can't: warmth, humor, honesty, pace.

We built the veil on purpose — so the first thing you fall for is the person, not the picture.
