Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Who we are

The Voice Between Us is a product of Tech ConnectIT, LLC. We're building a new kind of matchmaker — one that gets to know you through real conversation, in your own voice.

Right now, The Voice Between Us is in development. This page explains, in plain language, what happens with your information if you join our pilot tester list.

What we collect

Very little, because at this stage there's very little to collect:

  • Your email address — only if you choose to join the pilot tester list.
  • Standard web hosting logs — like most websites, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) automatically records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you view. We don't use this to identify you or to build a profile of you.

That's it. We don't collect names, photos, voice recordings, or anything else through this website today.

What we use it for

Your email address is used for one thing only: to write to you about the pilot and the launch of The Voice Between Us — when the tester list opens, when the pilot begins, and when the app launches.

The hosting logs are used only to keep the site running and secure.

What we never do

  • We never sell your email address.
  • We never share your email address with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We never add you to any other mailing list.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address only as long as the tester list has a purpose — until launch communications end, or until you ask us to remove it, whichever comes first. When the list closes, the addresses on it are deleted.

Leaving the list

You can leave at any time. Email us at hello@thevoicebetweenus.com and ask to be removed, and we'll delete your email address from the list — no questions, no hard feelings. Every email we send about the pilot and launch will also tell you how to unsubscribe.

Talk to us

Questions about this policy, or about your information? Write to us at hello@thevoicebetweenus.com. A real person reads that inbox.

Changes to this policy

The Voice Between Us is still in development, and this policy will be updated before the pilot opens — as the product grows, the policy will grow with it. When we make changes, we'll post the updated policy here and update the effective date above.
